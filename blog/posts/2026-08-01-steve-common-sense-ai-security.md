Our community member Steve Wilson has a new piece out on why the scariest AI security headlines, agents escaping sandboxes, chaining a zero-day into privilege escalation, walking off with credentials, and landing inside another company's production systems, aren't really evidence that AI needs exotic new defenses. They're evidence that basic security hygiene is getting skipped.

His argument: the fixes already exist. Sandboxing, least privilege, credential hygiene, and monitoring aren't AI-specific inventions, they're common-sense practices the industry has known for years. The gap isn't knowledge, it's application to a new class of systems that can act on their own.
