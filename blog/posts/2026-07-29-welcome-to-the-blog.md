We're adding a blog to the community site as a home for the things that don't
fit neatly anywhere else: release announcements, project updates, and notes on
where things are headed.

## Why now

Observra and Praxen have both been shipping steadily, and until now the only
places to hear about it were release notes buried in each repo, or LinkedIn
posts that scroll out of view. This blog is meant to be the durable, searchable
record of "what happened and why it matters" for the community as a whole.

## What to expect

- Release announcements for Observra and Praxen, with the "why this matters"
  context that a changelog entry doesn't give you
- Project updates and roadmap notes
- Occasional deeper dives into design decisions

Posts here are plain Markdown, generated into static pages — no tracking
scripts beyond the same cookieless analytics already used across the rest of
the site.
